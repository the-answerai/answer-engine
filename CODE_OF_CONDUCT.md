# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Answer Engine community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus discussion on the work.
- Respect privacy. Never post another person's credentials, private data,
  transcripts, or identifying information without permission.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a project decision.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, impersonation, and publishing private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, project
events, and public spaces where someone represents the project or its
community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact
method on [the-answerai organization profile](https://github.com/the-answerai).
Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Security
vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers will acknowledge a report, review available evidence, avoid
unnecessary disclosure, and take proportionate action. Possible actions
include a private warning, content removal, a temporary interaction limit, or
a permanent ban. Anyone directly involved in a report will recuse themselves
from deciding its outcome when another maintainer is available.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
